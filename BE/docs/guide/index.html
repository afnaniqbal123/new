Two things to know before you ship anything

The WebSocket handshake and the notifications SSE stream are both unauthenticated — they trust a client-supplied user id. And outside /auth/*, errors come back as HTTP 200 with the real status inside the body. Neither is a convention to copy; both are on the debt register with an intended fix.
